Events recorded this week:
- 2026-11-04 Oracle: Oracle drops a five-year deal, worth about $7bn, to rent Tencent about 100,000 AI chips in its Southeast Asia data centres, after US officials raised concerns and Chinese officials urged Tencent to buy Chinese chips
- 2026-11-06 Oracle: Microsoft declines to renew a 2023 deal to rent AI computing from Oracle and has been switching the capacity off; the servers ran older Nvidia chips. Oracle says demand for its AI infrastructure remains strong
- 2026-11-06 Microsoft: Microsoft is reviewing more than $80bn of contracts with outside cloud providers, including CoreWeave, Nscale and Nebius, and declined an option to expand an Nscale site in Texas
- 2026-11-06 CoreWeave: Yield on CoreWeave 2032 bonds tops 16%; five-year credit default swaps rise above 1,289bp on 5 Nov, above the October peak, against about 298bp for Oracle
- 2026-11-05 Data centres: ING reportedly puts about $500m of data centre loans up for sale, including loans tied to CyrusOne and Vantage and a slice tied to Oracle New Mexico Stargate site; bids due 19 Nov
- 2026-11-07 Anthropic: Anthropic presses ahead with its November listing but is willing to accept a lower valuation, targeting $1.25tn to $1.75tn (some investors expect about $1.3tn), and plans to raise $80bn to $100bn
