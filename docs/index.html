What the twelve signs are, and which are present

Signs of a late frenzy

  • ●The build-out is financed with debt rather than cash (Bank of England FPC, 25 Sep 2026; Barclays, 30 Sep 2026) present
  • ●Large commitments sit off balance sheet (Financial Times, 20 Sep 2026; Nikkei study, Aug 2026) present
  • ●Suppliers finance their own customers present
  • ●Record listings and products that bring in small savers present
  • ●Supervisors warn about the financing (Bank of England FPC, 25 Sep 2026) present
  • ●The market singles out the AI-dependent lenders present

Signs of a turning point

  • ○A frontier listing prices below its last private round absent
  • ○A key counterparty falls to speculative grade absent
  • ○Supplier financing rises while outside capital falls (needs at least two events of each kind in each half-year) absent
  • ○The lenders' gap widens beyond every year since 2024 absent
  • ○Oracle, CoreWeave and SoftBank all fall over three months absent
  • ○Defaults or redemption limits in AI financing vehicles absent

Four positions on the AI listings

Like the four points of a compass: each position is one of the paper's listing paths, with who holds it, what they have at stake and what the data show for it now. Bets last recorded 2026-09-29.

Heading: centre. The signals balance out: the evidence has not chosen a path yet. The trail of past headings will appear as updates accumulate. The needle adds up the explicit signals listed in each position below; it is a count of evidence, not a probability.

Strong listing

A public price above the private one

1 signal

Wins if the listings open above the last private rounds: the risk spreads to public shareholders and the tenants gain capital to pay their suppliers.

Main characters

  • Anthropic: Filed a confidential prospectus in June; listing expected after the November midterms (Forge Global; Reuters via TechSpot, 2 Oct 2026)
  • Polymarket traders: Priced Anthropic to list before OpenAI at 87% in July (Yahoo Finance, July 2026)
  • Pre-IPO funds: Hold shares before the listing, some open to small investors (one over 20% in Anthropic) (The Motley Fool, Sep 2026)
  • The shareholders of the tenants: SoftBank (~13% of OpenAI), Microsoft (27%), Amazon and Alphabet (Anthropic) would be marked up (Reuters, 1 Oct 2026; Microsoft, 28 Oct 2025)

What the data show now

  • Anthropic has filed to list

Weak listing

Private prices are not public prices

2 signals

Wins if a listing prices below the last round or falls after it: the doubt about payment reaches SoftBank first, then Oracle, CoreWeave and the chip suppliers.

Main characters

  • Michael Burry: Put options on Nvidia, Oracle, the semiconductor index and Applied Materials, expiring June 2027 (CNBC, 28 Sep 2026)
  • Banks financing Oracle: Buy default insurance on Oracle to hedge their project loans (Fast Company, 23 May 2026)
  • Oracle and CoreWeave: Not bettors but the most exposed: over $300bn and $22.4bn contracted with OpenAI; S&P cut Oracle to BBB- in July (Table 3 of the paper; S&P, 9 Jul 2026)

What the data show now

  • A rating agency has cut a key counterparty
  • The market singles out the AI-dependent lenders

Delay

Private money pays more and asks less

2 signals

Wins if the listings slip: private rounds keep valuations untested, and the risk stays with Oracle, CoreWeave and SoftBank.

Main characters

  • OpenAI and Sam Altman: No listing in 2026; seeking at least $30bn privately at about $1.4tn (Bloomberg via CoinDesk, 30 Sep 2026)
  • Polymarket traders: Gave 79.5% to no OpenAI listing in 2026 in July (Yahoo Finance, July 2026)
  • SoftBank: Completed its $30bn investment, partly with bonds; its stake stays without a public price (Reuters, 1 Oct 2026)

What the data show now

  • OpenAI is raising privately instead of listing
  • No OpenAI listing has been priced

Market fall

Stop the listings before they start

1 signal

Wins if a broad fall stops the listings: the tenants then depend on suppliers and private credit, and the AI-dependent lenders lag most.

Main characters

  • Michael Burry: Wants markets to fall hard enough to stop the OpenAI and Anthropic listings (Benzinga, 30 Sep 2026)
  • The Bank of England: Not a bettor but a watcher: warns that several vulnerabilities could crystallise together (FPC record, 25 Sep 2026)

What the data show now

  • The 10-year yield rose more than 50 basis points in three months
