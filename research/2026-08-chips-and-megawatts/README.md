# Chips and Megawatts (August 2026)

*Chips and Megawatts: structural attribution in the AI supply chain, 2015-2026.* Alberto Acedo. SSRN working paper 7307362,
August 2026. https://ssrn.com/abstract=7307362

Over a decade, structural weight in the AI supply chain moved from the buyers of computing power to their suppliers, above
all the makers of electrical equipment. The October paper builds on this result.

The code and data released with this paper remain where they were published, in the repository
github.com/BiomeMakers/TSI-OmegaS (folder ai-chain), so that the links in the paper keep working.
