# Chips and Megawatts (August 2026)

*Chips and Megawatts: a network measurement of the AI supply chain.* Alberto Acedo. SSRN working paper 7307362,
19 August 2026. https://ssrn.com/abstract=7307362

Over a decade, structural weight in the AI supply chain moved from the buyers of computing power to their suppliers, above
all the makers of electrical equipment and power. The September note and the October paper in this repository build on
this result.

`tickers_panel_agosto.txt` lists the companies of the August panel. The analysis code of the August paper was not released
with it; it will be added to this folder when it is. Raw prices are not redistributed.
